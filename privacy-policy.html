Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

Ivan Posting collects the following information:

  • Account credentials: Encrypted TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram OAuth tokens necessary for posting content
  • Content data: Videos, images, captions, and metadata you choose to publish
  • Usage data: Basic analytics about posting activity for service improvement

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to:

  • Authenticate with social media platforms on your behalf
  • Publish content you create to your connected accounts
  • Maintain and improve the Service

3. Data Storage and Security

All credentials are stored locally on your machine in encrypted form. We do not store your data on external servers. OAuth tokens are transmitted directly to platform APIs (TikTok, Meta) for authentication purposes only.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Content is only shared to platforms you explicitly authorize.

5. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with third-party platforms (TikTok, Facebook, Instagram). Your use of those platforms is subject to their respective privacy policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy

6. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as you use the Service. You may delete your data at any time by removing the local credential files.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
